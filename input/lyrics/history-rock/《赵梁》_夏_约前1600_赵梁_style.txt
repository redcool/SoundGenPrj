# 《赵梁》双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
dark industrial rock, sleek and poisonous; 116 bpm, gliding mid-tempo groove with metallic hits; low synth bass, detuned guitar stabs, guqin single notes, faint wine-pool ripple and palace ambience in the background; low male lead, low register, smooth and close-mic, insinuating; dry, close, polished yet dirty production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
industrial metal, cold and predatory; 124 bpm, mechanical four on the floor with war-drum accents; down-tuned riff, metallic percussion, bianzhong stabs, low drone; clipped male lead, dry and level, faintly mocking; tight, dense, modern production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
