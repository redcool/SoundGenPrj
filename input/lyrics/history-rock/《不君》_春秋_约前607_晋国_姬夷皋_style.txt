# 《不君》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
dark industrial rock, petulant and violent, a spoiled child with power; 114 bpm, heavy mid-tempo with war-drum hits; detuned guitar riff, tolling bianzhong, low strings, distant palace crowd murmur; low male lead, mid register, sneering and close-mic; dry, close, unpolished production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
doom metal, grim and suffocating; 100 bpm, crawling half-time with a tolling bell; down-tuned doom riff, sparse bronze bell, low bowed bass; deep male lead, growl-edged and weary; wide, cavernous, dense production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
