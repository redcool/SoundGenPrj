# 《自荐》 双卡（五要素完整版）

symphonic metal, heroic and tense; 126 bpm, double kick with strings and war drum; orchestral strings, brass, guitar harmonies, bianzhong; strong male lead, high-mid, heroic; wide, bright, cinematic production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
