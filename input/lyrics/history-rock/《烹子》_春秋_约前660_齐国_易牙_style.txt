# 《烹子》双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
dark industrial rock, cold and insidious, with a kitchen-fire unease; 112 bpm, heavy slow four on the floor with metallic percussion; low detuned guitar riff, tolling bianzhong, guqin single notes, simmering pot and fire ambience in the background; low male lead, low register, hushed and close-mic, oily and unhurried; dry, close, unpolished production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
doom metal, oppressive and inevitable; 88 bpm, crawling half-time with a tolling bell; down-tuned doom riff, sparse bronze bell, low bowed strings, distant crowd murmur; deep male lead, growl-edged but controlled, deliberate; wide, dense, cavernous production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
