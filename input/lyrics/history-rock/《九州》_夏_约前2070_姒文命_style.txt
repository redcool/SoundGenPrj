# 《九州》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
uplifting trance, vast and stately; 120 bpm, rolling bassline with a long build; shimmering synth arpeggio over guzheng plucks and xun flute, wide pads, soft kick; calm male lead, mid register, restrained and clear; clean, spacious, airy production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
techno, steady and monumental; 118 bpm, four on the floor with a driving bass; low synth bass, guzheng motif, bianzhong hits, analog pad layers; steady male lead, low register, unadorned; dry, tight, club-forward production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
