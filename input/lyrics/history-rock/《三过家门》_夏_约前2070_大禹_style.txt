# 《三过家门》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
Chinese epic heavy metal, slow and burdened, with a flood-scale weight; 108 bpm, marching double kick; down-tuned electric guitar riff with tolling bianzhong, deep dagu war drum, low bowed bass drone, distant water roar in the background; deep male lead, low register, weathered and restrained, close-mic; dry, wide, unpolished production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
symphonic metal, solemn and mournful; 96 bpm, slow half-time with a rising string swell; low strings, guzheng arpeggio, heavy guitar chug, sparse bronze bell hits, faint flood ambience; solemn male baritone, measured phrasing, aged and story-worn; wide chamber reverb, natural production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
