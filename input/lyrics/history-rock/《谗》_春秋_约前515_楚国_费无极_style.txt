# 《谗》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
trip-hop, whispering and sinister; 96 bpm, slow broken beat with sub pulse; low sub bass, guzheng plucks, reversed strings, close whisper texture; male lead, breathy and half-spoken, insinuating; dark, reverberant, intimate production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
dark art rock, quiet menace; 112 bpm, steady slow pulse with a knife-edge build; solo guqin, low bowed strings, sparse percussion, palace wind; male lead, low and soft, controlled; dry, close, unpolished production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
