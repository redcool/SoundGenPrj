# 《日食》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
chamber rock, small and powerless, with a quiet dread; 112 bpm, slow steady beat with a thin pulse; solo guqin, low bowed strings, sparse bianzhong, faint crowd drumming far away; soft male lead, mid register, hesitant and close-mic; dry, intimate, unpolished production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
ambient art rock, hollow and suspended; 96 bpm, near-static pulse with a slow swell; wide pads, distant xun flute, bowed bass, faint eclipse-like drone; quiet male lead, breathy, almost spoken; wide, reverberant, spacious production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
