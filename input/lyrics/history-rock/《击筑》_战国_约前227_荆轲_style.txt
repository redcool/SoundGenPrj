# 《击筑》双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
dark assassin rock, bleak and resolute; 96 bpm, slow heavy march with wind noise; distorted guitar, cello, plucked zither evoking the zhu, heavy drums, cold river wind ambience; low male lead, low register, cold and restrained, half-spoken in the parting lines; wide, dry, outdoor production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
symphonic metal, heroic and doomed; 112 bpm, driving double kick with a mournful string theme; orchestral strings, cello lead, guzheng, distorted guitars, war drum; strong male lead, mid register, controlled and tragic; wide, cinematic, reverberant production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
