# 《堰》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
Chinese folk rock, steady and patient; 108 bpm, flowing mid-tempo with water-like percussion; acoustic guitar, guzheng, dizi, low drum, river water ambience; warm male lead, mid register, plain and close-mic; natural, spacious, dry production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
ambient art rock, vast and calm; 96 bpm, slow pulse with flowing layers; wide pads, guzheng arpeggio, xun flute, bowed bass, water texture; soft male lead, low register, unhurried; wide, reverberant, spacious production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
