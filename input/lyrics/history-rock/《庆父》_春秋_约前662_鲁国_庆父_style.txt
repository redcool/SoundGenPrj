# 《庆父》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
dark industrial rock, funereal and oppressive; 114 bpm, slow heavy march with funeral bell; low detuned riff, tolling bianzhong, guqin single notes, distant mourning ambience; low male lead, low register, hollow and close-mic; dry, cavernous, unpolished production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
doom metal, relentless gloom; 92 bpm, crawling half-time with a bell; down-tuned doom riff, low bowed strings, sparse bell, wind; deep male lead, growl-edged, weary; wide, dense, muddy production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
