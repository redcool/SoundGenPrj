# 《负荆》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
Chinese epic heavy rock, blunt and proud, then humbled; 116 bpm, heavy war-drum march; distorted guitar riff, war drum, bianzhong accents, low brass, distant camp ambience; strong male lead, mid register, gruff and close-mic; wide, punchy, dry production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
symphonic metal, regal and elegiac; 104 bpm, slow heavy march with strings; orchestral strings, cello, guzheng, guitar chug; deep male baritone, aged and restrained; wide, cinematic, reverberant production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
