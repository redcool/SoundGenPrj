# 《射日》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
power metal, heroic and blazing; 156 bpm, galloping double kick; twin electric guitars in harmony, tolling bianzhong accents, guzheng flourishes, low brass swells, distant thunder background; high male lead, clear and controlled, heroic but not screamed; bright, punchy, arena-wide production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
groove metal, dark and mythic; 138 bpm, heavy syncopated groove with war drums; down-tuned riff, war drum, suona stab, low brass drone, crackling fire ambience in background; mid-range male lead, aggressive but controlled, dry delivery; raw, close, dense production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
