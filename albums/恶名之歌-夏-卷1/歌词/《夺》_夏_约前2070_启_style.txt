# 《夺》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
epic heavy rock, triumphant but uneasy; 118 bpm, heavy marching groove; distorted guitar riff, bianzhong tolling, low brass, war drum, crowd-distance cheer ambience; strong male lead, mid register, controlled and commanding; wide, punchy, outdoor production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
industrial rock, cold and calculating; 124 bpm, machine-like four on the floor with metallic hits; low synth bass, detuned guitar, metallic percussion, sparse bell; clipped male lead, dry and level; tight, close, modern production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
