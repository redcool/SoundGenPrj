# 《袖》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
dark art rock, sweet then poisonous; 112 bpm, slow pulse with a knife-edge build; solo guqin, low bowed strings, jade-ring percussion, palace wind ambience; female lead, mid register, soft and close-mic, controlled menace; dry, intimate, unpolished production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
trip-hop, suspended and sinister; 96 bpm, slow broken beat with sub pulse; low sub bass, guzheng plucks, reversed strings, whisper texture; female lead, breathy and low, half-spoken; dark, reverberant, intimate production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
