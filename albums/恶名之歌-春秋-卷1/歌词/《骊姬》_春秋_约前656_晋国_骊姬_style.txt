# 《骊姬》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
dark art rock, intimate and poisonous; 112 bpm, slow steady pulse with a knife-edge build; solo guqin, low bowed strings, sparse bianzhong, faint palace wind ambience; female lead, mid register, soft and close-mic, controlled menace; dry, intimate, unpolished production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
trip-hop, suspended and sinister; 96 bpm, slow broken beat with a deep sub pulse; low sub bass, guzheng plucks, reversed strings, distant whisper texture; female lead, breathy and low, half-spoken in verses; wide, reverberant, dark production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
