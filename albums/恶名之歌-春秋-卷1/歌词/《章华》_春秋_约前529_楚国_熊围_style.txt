# 《章华》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
dark epic rock, arrogant then hollow; 112 bpm, heavy groove with bronze-bell accents; distorted guitar, tolling bianzhong, guzheng, low strings, distant wind on a terrace; low male lead, commanding at first then breaking; wide, punchy, spacious production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
techno, cold monumentality; 118 bpm, four on the floor with metallic hits; low synth bass, bianzhong stabs, guzheng motif, pad layers; level male lead, dry and distant; tight, modern, dry production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
