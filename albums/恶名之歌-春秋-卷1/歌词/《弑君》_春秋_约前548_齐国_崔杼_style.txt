# 《弑君》双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
dark industrial rock, tense and claustrophobic; 116 bpm, heavy mid-tempo with metallic accents; low detuned guitar riff, tolling bianzhong, sparse guqin, brush of bamboo slips and distant rain ambience; low male lead, low register, controlled and close-mic, cold; dry, close, unpolished production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
doom metal, grim and inevitable; 92 bpm, crawling half-time with a tolling bell; down-tuned doom riff, low bowed strings, sparse bronze bell, faint wind; deep male lead, growl-edged but controlled; wide, cavernous, dense production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
