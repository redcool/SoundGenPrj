# 《火牛》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
war rock, desperate and explosive; 120 bpm, driving double kick with war drums; distorted guitars, war drum, bronze bell, fire crackle and stampede ambience; fierce male lead, mid register, urgent; raw, wide, loud production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
industrial rock, night-raid tension; 126 bpm, machine-like groove with sudden bursts; detuned riff, metallic hits, low synth drone, distant horns; clipped male lead, dry and hard; dense, compressed, tight production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
