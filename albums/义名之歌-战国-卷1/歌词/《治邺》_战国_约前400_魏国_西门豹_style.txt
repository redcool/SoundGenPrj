# 《治邺》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
Chinese folk rock, wry and resolute; 112 bpm, rolling mid-tempo with river percussion; acoustic guitar, guzheng, dizi, hand drum, river water ambience; warm male lead, mid register, plain and firm; natural, dry, spacious production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
art rock, stern and ceremonial; 104 bpm, slow procession with bells; bianzhong, low strings, guqin, sparse hand drum, water texture; solemn male baritone, measured; wide, reverberant production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
