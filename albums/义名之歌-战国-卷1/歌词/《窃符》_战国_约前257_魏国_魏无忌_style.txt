# 《窃符》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
Chinese epic rock, urgent and morally torn; 114 bpm, driving march with war-drum accents; distorted guitar, bianzhong, guzheng, low strings, night rain ambience; clear male lead, mid register, resolute and close-mic; wide, punchy, dry production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
symphonic metal, heroic and costly; 120 bpm, double kick with a rising string theme; orchestral strings, war drum, guzheng, guitar harmonies; strong male lead, high-mid, controlled; wide, cinematic, reverberant production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
