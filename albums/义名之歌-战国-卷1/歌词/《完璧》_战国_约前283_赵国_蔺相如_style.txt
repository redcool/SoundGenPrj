# 《完璧》 双卡（五要素完整版）

## A（v 卡）
Chinese epic rock, tense and defiant; 114 bpm, mid-tempo march with palace-drum accents; distorted guitar, tolling bianzhong, guqin, low strings, hall ambience in the background; clear male lead, mid register, controlled and firm, close-mic; wide, punchy, dry production, Mandarin Chinese vocals

## B（b 卡）
symphonic metal, heroic and ceremonial; 122 bpm, driving double kick with bronze accents; orchestral strings, bianzhong, electric guitar harmonies, war drum; strong male lead, high-mid register, heroic but not screamed; wide, bright, cinematic production, Mandarin Chinese vocals
