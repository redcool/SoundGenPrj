Oriental Progressive Art Rock, Epic Folk Rock, 92 BPM, Deep Warm Male Baritone Vocals. Chinese bamboo flute and acoustic guitar as the main melodic instruments. A fusion of Eastern traditional music and Western melodic rock. Warm nostalgic verses with storytelling vocals, gradually building into powerful, emotional and anthemic choruses with distorted electric guitars, deep bass, live drums and cinematic orchestral strings. Expressive bamboo flute and electric guitar instrumental solo. The bridge becomes intimate and philosophical with soft piano and lonely bamboo flute. The final chorus reaches an epic, bittersweet climax, expressing friendship, brotherhood, separation, memories and inner strength. The outro fades into a solitary bamboo flute melody. Nostalgic, heroic, melancholic, warm and profound.
