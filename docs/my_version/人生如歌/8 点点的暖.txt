East-West Fusion Cinematic Art Rock, 88 BPM. Deep mature male baritone vocals, warm, soulful and weathered. Lead instruments: expressive concert flute and electric pipa, blending Eastern elegance with Western cinematic rock. Supporting electric guitar, deep bass and Chinese tanggu drums. Rich atmospheric reverb, expansive widescreen soundstage and immersive depth. Gentle, intimate storytelling verses with delicate pipa picking and lyrical flute melodies. Warm, memorable and uplifting anthemic choruses, gradually building with powerful drums, soaring guitars and orchestral layers. Reflective piano and flute bridge, followed by an emotional grand final chorus. End with a peaceful flute and electric pipa duet. Compassionate, bittersweet, hopeful, humane and deeply moving. Natural Chinese vocal phrasing, no rap, no EDM.
