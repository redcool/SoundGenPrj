Dark Chinese folk-rock elegy, slow 68-72 BPM, minor pentatonic. Muted palm-muted electric guitar arpeggios and a deep electric bass drone under xiao flute and xun ocarina wails; sparse guzheng; heavy slow low bass drum hits like wheel ruts on frozen ground; cello and low strings; a restrained clean electric guitar swell only at the hook, no solo. Male mid-low vocal in classical Chinese, chest-storytelling with breathy restraint, controlled dynamics. Mood: starving exiles in a blizzard, devotion expressed as restraint - the flesh offered in a bowl of soup. Wuxia melancholy, cinematic space, 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling vocals, no death metal, no autotune, no EDM drops, no upbeat major key, no rap.
