Misty Chinese rock ballad of comfortable decay, slow-mid 68-74 BPM. Hazy clean electric guitar chords and electric bass lay a velvet bed; guzheng sparkles like wine light; bamboo dizi lulls; soft drums only hint; the chorus opens the window with a low male hum and one restrained guitar swell. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, drowsy, sighing, half-drunk charm with breathy edges; no belting. Mood: five golden years of forgetting - a hero rusting in silk, brothers restless, ambition dissolving in cups - and the faint alarm bell underneath the warmth. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
