Dark, unsettling Chinese art-rock piece, mid-slow 70-76 BPM. Discordant clean electric guitar harmonics and a suspicious electric bass line sketch a peeping scene; guzheng tremolo like a raised curtain; xun hollow and exposed; soft EDM ticks like dripping water; big drum hits only at choruses, clipped short. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, cold controlled anger under a flat narration; a low male choir shadows the hook. Mood: a king peeping through a curtain at an exile's body - absurd, humiliating, unforgettable - the ledger of slights gaining a page that will be repaid with interest. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no heavy distortion, no autotune, no party pop.
