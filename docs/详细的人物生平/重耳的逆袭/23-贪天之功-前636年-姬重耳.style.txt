Cold minimal Chinese art-rock narrative, 68-72 BPM. Sparse guzheng harmonics, xiao and guqin lines over a deep electric bass pulse; clean-toned electric guitar figures thin and cold; bianzhong bell accents; a low ceremonial drum presses in only at choruses, then everything falls back to a solo flute. Male mid-low vocal in classical Chinese, cold and clear like quiet recitation, breathy and controlled. Mood: one man's silence against a hall full of clamoring reward-seekers - court noise versus mountain quiet as the arrangement's breathing. Wuxia restraint over a modern rhythm section, 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no heavy distortion, no EDM drops, no autotune, no rap, no choir stacking.
