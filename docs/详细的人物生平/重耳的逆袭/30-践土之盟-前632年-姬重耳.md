# 践土之盟(前632年 · 姬重耳)

## 元数据头

- 事件线:吕郤之乱(主歌A)+ 勤王尊周(主歌A)+ 践土之盟(主歌B/副歌)+ 逝世(尾声)(歌单 10 号,全集终章)
- 时间:前636—前628年
- 体量:38 行;一韵到底(中东辙)
- 立意:九年三步——平乱立信、勤王立义、践土立霸;登顶与落幕同曲,霸业有尽,信义长存。

## Suno Style 块

- 曲风:加冕级史诗恢弘,渐强;尾声转苍凉低语
- 乐器:编钟、战鼓、大鼓、弦乐群、低音铜管;尾声埙独奏
- 人声:男声中低音庄重叙事,副歌男女合唱托举,尾声近私语,全程克制
- 推导理由:①平乱勤王的处境,取行进感的庄严;②践土册命的场景,以编钟与合唱写大典;③逝世的落点,收进苍凉低语,霸业与坟茔对照
- 时长:3-4 分钟(3-4 minutes)
- 负面提示:无电子合成器、无失真电吉他、无说唱、无英文、无嘶吼、无欢快律动
- 英文 style(Suno v6 完整版·武侠摇滚修订,粘 Style 框):Grand wuxia Chinese epic-rock coronation, building 76-88 BPM. Bianzhong bells and low bass drum open with ritual weight; electric guitars and electric bass join war drums; electric pipa and guzheng answer in courtly dialogue; bamboo dizi and suona crown the choruses; EDM textures and risers lift the mandate scene; the final verses drop to a near-whisper over a solo xun ocarina as snow falls on the coffin city. Male mid-low vocal in classical Chinese, dignified narrative, male-female choir rising without screaming. Mood: nine years in three steps - survive the palace fire, honor the Son of Heaven, accept the mandate, then the coffin at Quwo. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal vocals, no autotune, no party pop.
