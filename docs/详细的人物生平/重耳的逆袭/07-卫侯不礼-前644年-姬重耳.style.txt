Cold Chinese art-rock ballad of humiliation, mid-slow 70-76 BPM. Clean-toned electric guitar figures ring cold; electric bass walks a weary line; guzheng sparse like blowing dust; a distant low bass drum marks footsteps out of the gate; no big chorus - restraint everywhere, a subtle EDM pulse like city drums far off. Male solo vocal in classical Chinese, dry and unhurried, narrating slights like ledger entries; brief low male hum on the turnaround. Mood: a fallen prince ignored by a petty king - cold eyes, empty markets, dust and hunger - pride ground like a blade on stone, and the lesson banked for nineteen years. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no heavy distortion, no autotune, no rap, no party pop.
