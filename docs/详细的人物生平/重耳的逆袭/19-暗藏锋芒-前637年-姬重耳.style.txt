Smoldering Chinese wuxia rock, mid-tempo 76-84 BPM, restraint as power. Guqin and bamboo dizi open over a deep electric bass pulse; electric guitars stay coiled in clean pentatonic lines until the chorus, where they bloom without distortion; electric pipa flickers like a blade edge; big drum and bianzhong answer; a subtle EDM riser lifts the farewell. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, courteous surface with iron underneath, controlled dynamics throughout. Mood: a banquet of tests answered with one measured promise - three stages of retreat - pride sheathed, credit banked, a future battlefield foreshadowed in a toast. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
