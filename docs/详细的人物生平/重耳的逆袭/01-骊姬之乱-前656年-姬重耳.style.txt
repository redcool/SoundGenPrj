Dark Chinese palace-conspiracy rock, slow-to-mid 70-78 BPM, minor pentatonic. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, pressed and narrative; female harmony icy and close. Mood: an old king's favor turned into a weapon - night tears, poisoned sacrifice, princes scattered, a court full of unseen knives. Wuxia menace, cinematic and cold, 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
