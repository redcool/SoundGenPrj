Vindicated Chinese wuxia rock, mid-tempo 78-86 BPM. War drums and electric bass open the campaign; electric guitars bite in pentatonic riffs; electric pipa shrieks like siege horns; se zither holds the old grudge motif; suona calls the breached gate; the mercy verses drop to muted strings and a lone dizi. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, hard in battle, level in judgment; low male choir answers like an army hearing orders. Mood: old humiliations repaid in full order - the peeping king captured, the kind host's gate untouched, an officer executed for the rules - revenge with a ledger, not a torch. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal vocals, no autotune, no party pop.
