Anthemic wuxia Chinese rock, mid-tempo 78-84 BPM. Guqin and bamboo dizi open over warm strings; electric guitar riffs and electric bass groove build from the first chorus; guzheng runs sparkle above; low bass drum keeps a marching pulse; an EDM-style riser and subtle programmed layer lift the final chorus without burying the acoustic roots. Male mid-low vocal in classical Chinese, murmuring breathy verses, firm open final chorus, restrained power throughout. Mood: a drunk farewell that was a conspiracy of love - four directions calling, a long wind rising as escort. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no cheerful party pop.
