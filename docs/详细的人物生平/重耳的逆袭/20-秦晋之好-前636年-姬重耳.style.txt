Epic wuxia Chinese rock anthem, building from about 72 to 96 BPM. Xun ocarina and low strings open in exile dusk; electric guitars and electric bass enter with war drums and low bass drum; electric pipa duels the electric guitar in Qin-versus-Jin answering phrases; guzheng and bamboo dizi keep the folk heart; suona crowns the final chorus; an EDM-style riser lifts the last hook. Baritone lead vocal in classical Chinese with one clear female answer in the pre-chorus, joined choir rising without screaming. Mood: the wanderer's exile ending in alliance, gratitude and pride held in check. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal vocals, no autotune, no party pop.
