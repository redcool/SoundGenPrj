Tense Chinese wuxia rock thriller, mid-tempo 78-86 BPM. Heartbeat drum and electric bass pulse through a night chase; electric pipa shrieks warning phrases; palm-muted electric guitars drive the pursuit verses; bamboo dizi sobs the steppe farewell; suona faintly warns from far walls; chorus swells with big drum but stays controlled. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, pressed and urgent, breathy verses, controlled power on the hook. Mood: the same assassin sent twice, a lifetime learned in one night - enemies sharpen your edge, and mercy to the blade-bearer becomes statecraft years later. Dark, kinetic, never frantic, 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
