Anthemic Chinese wuxia rock, mid-tempo 76-84 BPM, group-portrait energy. Guzheng runs and bamboo dizi open; electric guitars enter in clean pentatonic phrases that grow into a full riff; electric bass drives a marching groove; low bass drum and war drums answer like a mustering host; an EDM-style riser lifts the final chorus. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese with male choir answering each name like a roll call - strategists, scholars, warriors - restrained but rising. Mood: a prince's exile team becoming a shadow government - loyalty forged in snow and hunger, talent gathering around character, not riches. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
