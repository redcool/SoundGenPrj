Quiet Chinese post-rock elegy, slow 68 BPM, fire-banned stillness. Bamboo xiao flute leads hollow and cold; ambient clean electric guitar swells and a soft electric bass sustain under sparse guzheng; soft mallet pulses tick like a water clock; no drums until a low bass drum heartbeat appears late and fades away. Breathy baritone vocal in classical Chinese, near a monologue; soft low unified harmonies on choruses, like a nation mourning with voices pressed down. Mood: an emperor's private remorse - cold smoke, cold food, remembrance rather than accusation, one cold wick for one loyal friend. Spacious reverb, long silences, 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no metal, no EDM beats, no autotune, no rap, no suona, no belting.
