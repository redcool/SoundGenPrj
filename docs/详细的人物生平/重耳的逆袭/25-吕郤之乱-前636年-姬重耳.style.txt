Tense Chinese wuxia rock of fire and mercy, mid-tempo 76-84 BPM. Restless electric bass and tight drum grooves portray the plot; electric guitar stabs like alarms; electric pipa scratches nervous phrases; the palace-fire chorus opens into heavy-but-controlled riffing; then everything drops to a whisper for the amnesty verse over solo xun. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, sharp in the confrontation, serene in the pardon; low male choir answers like the court. Mood: an assassin's knock saves a throne - the past enemy trusted, the conspirators outfoxed, clemency mightier than the blade. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
