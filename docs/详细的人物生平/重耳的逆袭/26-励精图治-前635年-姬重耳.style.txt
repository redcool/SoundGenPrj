Constructive Chinese epic-rock statecraft anthem, steady 76-82 BPM. Open fifth drones and guzheng runs like rebuilding markets; electric guitar arpeggios orderly and rising; electric bass and marching drum give productive momentum; bamboo dizi threads the fields; choruses add full strings and a low choir like edicts read to the people; light EDM pulse keeps modern urgency. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, firm, almost teacherly, hopeful without bombast. Mood: nineteen years of exile turned into policy - open trade, rested farmers, merit above birth, the Three Armies and Six Ministers born - a nation reversing its decay. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
