Warm Chinese folk-rock ballad of honor, mid-tempo 74-80 BPM. Nylon-string guitar and guzheng open like a candlelit room; electric bass warm and steady; electric guitar answers with a humble clean phrase; soft rock drums enter at choruses with low male choir pad; a short suona distant call hints the future repayment. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, dignified and grateful, controlled warmth. Mood: a hidden jade under a gift of rice at midnight - the meal accepted, the treasure returned - integrity priced in hungry years, rewarded by an untouched gate in a conquered city. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
