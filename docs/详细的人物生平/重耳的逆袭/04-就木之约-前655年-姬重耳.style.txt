Tender Chinese folk-rock ballad of steppe love, mid-slow 68-76 BPM. Nylon-string guitar and guzheng arpeggios open in wide steppe light; bamboo dizi sings the love theme; electric bass sways beneath; a warm reggae-tinged offbeat drifts through the verses; choruses add soft rock drums and low male choir pad; the outro fades to a lone xun. Male mid-low vocal in classical Chinese, husky and warm; a clear female voice answers as the steppe wife, both restrained. Mood: twelve gentle years in exile, a twenty-five-year vow, a goodbye at the felt tents - love that becomes a wall of waiting. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
