## 1 骊姬之乱

```
Dark Chinese palace-conspiracy rock, slow-to-mid 70-78 BPM, minor pentatonic. Low strings and a heartbeat drum build dread under the throne room; electric bass pulses like scheming; electric pipa flickers venomous little phrases; a shadowy female whisper vocal threads the verses like the scheming consort; guzheng and xun keep the ancient frame; big drum hits land only at choruses, restrained. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, pressed and narrative; female harmony icy and close. Mood: an old king's favor turned into a weapon - night tears, poisoned sacrifice, princes scattered, a court full of unseen knives. Wuxia menace, cinematic and cold, 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
```
