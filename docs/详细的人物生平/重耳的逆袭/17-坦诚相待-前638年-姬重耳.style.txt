Noble Chinese rock ballad of respect between defeated men, mid-tempo 74-80 BPM. Solo guqin and bamboo dizi open like a wounded king's hall; warm strings and clean electric guitar swell gently; electric bass steady; soft war drum heartbeat; the chorus lifts with a low male choir, dignified, never bombastic. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, steady and moved, controlled power; a brief lower narration voice as the lame old duke. Mood: a defeated ruler still keeping full rites for a passing exile - twenty horses of farewell - respect as an investment that returns years later as an army. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
