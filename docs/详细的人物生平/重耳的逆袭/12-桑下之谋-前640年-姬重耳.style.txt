Intimate Chinese wuxia rock conspiracy ballad, mid-slow 72-78 BPM. Solo se zither and electric bass pulse whisper under mulberry leaves; muted electric guitar ghosts the melody; a soft heartbeat drum builds; electric pipa plucks secret signals; the chorus lifts with low strings and choir breath, never loud. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, low urgent narration; a warm female voice as the wife-conspirator, resolute; duet restraint throughout. Mood: the gentlest plot ever hatched - a wife and an uncle drugging a drunk prince onto a cart at night, love disguised as betrayal, far-sight disguised as harshness. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal, no autotune, no party pop.
