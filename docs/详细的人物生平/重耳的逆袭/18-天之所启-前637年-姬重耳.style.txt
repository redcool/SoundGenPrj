Dark cautionary Chinese rock, mid-tempo 76-82 BPM. Tense se zither and clean electric guitar figures trade courtly phrases; electric bass prowls; bianzhong accents toll like warnings; big drum and low brass hit the chorus where the counselor pleads; an EDM-style riser under the final admonition. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, cold and observational; a stern lower voice as the advisor, clipped like decrees. Mood: a short-sighted king refusing both courtesy and counsel - heaven marks the man, arrogance writes the debt, and years later the bill arrives with an army. Wuxia gravity with modern rhythm, 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal vocals, no autotune, no party pop.
