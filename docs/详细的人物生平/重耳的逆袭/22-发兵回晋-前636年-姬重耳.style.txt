Triumphant Chinese wuxia epic-rock return, building 76-88 BPM. War drums, low bass drum and oars rhythm open on the river; electric guitars rise in pentatonic waves; electric bass drives the crossing; suona calls the landing; guzheng shimmers over the homeland gates; EDM textures lift the coronation verse. Male lead vocal in classical Chinese, weathered and full, chorus rising with male choir but controlled; the river-oath line half-spoken. Mood: a sixty-two-year-old prince coming home at the bow of an armed fleet - nineteen years of roads ending in one spring flood, old banners falling, ancestral bells ringing. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling, no death metal vocals, no autotune, no party pop.
