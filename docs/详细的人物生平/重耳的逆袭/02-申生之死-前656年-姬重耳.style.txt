Tragic Chinese rock elegy, slow 66-72 BPM. Sparse guqin and xun ocarina open in mourning; muted electric guitar arpeggios and deep electric bass enter like a slow procession; low bass drum tolls like a funeral bell at the choruses; a restrained electric guitar swell only at the hook, no solo. Male solo vocal in classical Chinese, low and grieving, controlled, a male choir humming beneath like mourning kin. Mood: a crown prince crushed between loyalty and slander - the poisoned meat, the silent father, the white silk in the ancestral hall, two younger brothers fleeing into the snow. Grief compressed, never wailing, 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling vocals, no death metal, no autotune, no upbeat rhythm.
