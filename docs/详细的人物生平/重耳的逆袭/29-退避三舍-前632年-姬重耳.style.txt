Wuxia Chinese metal epic, about 88 BPM, the album's battle peak. Low bass drum and taiko-weight toms form the skeleton; heavy electric guitars in pentatonic riffs answer se zither tension; electric pipa shrieks like battle horns; suona war-cries lead the charge; guqin and xun keep the cold promise motif; an EDM-style riser and programmed drum layer drive the final assault, resolving to a single bianzhong strike. Baritone solo vocal in classical Chinese, compressed and commanding, chorus rising without cracking, the final line dropping to a near-whisper. Mood: ninety miles kept as a promise, then the counter-charge - restraint winning before arms do. 3-4 minutes. No English vocals, no growling vocals, no death metal vocals, no autotune, no happy pop.
